Academic Workflow

How GSSS LMS Portal Works

A structured 4-step digital cycle built to reinforce daily classroom instruction, evaluate practical problem solving, and mentor candidates throughout their degree program.

01

Lecture Modules & Study Notes

Watch high-definition, curriculum-mapped video lessons prepared by GSSS faculty. Download chapter notes, PDFs, and reference materials anytime.

✓ Unit-wise Syllabus Mapping
02

Hands-On Practice & Compilers

Solve programming exercises in Python, C++, Java, and SQL with real-time compiler feedback. BBA & B.Com candidates solve practical case study drills.

✓ Live Test-Case Assertions
03

Proctored Mock Assessments

Take timed unit evaluations and Online Assessments (OAs) in a proctored environment equipped with strict anti-cheat tab-monitoring warnings.

✓ Tab-Switch Tracking & Integrity
04

Live Meets & Streaks

Join mentor-scheduled Google Meets, collaborate on cohort batch channels, and build daily study streaks on the 53-week heatmap.

✓ Real-time Doubt Support
Platform Capabilities

Essential LMS Tools for Students & Mentors

Built-in digital features engineered specifically to support continuous academic progression.

📅

53-Week Contribution Heatmap

Visualizes student daily activity, completed lectures, and coding submissions using GitHub-style activity squares to foster disciplined study habits.

🛡️

Anti-Cheat Mock Exam Engine

Monitors window visibility during timed assessments, records tab switch counts, and warns candidates in real-time to preserve exam integrity.

💬

WhatsApp Web-Style Batch Chat

Real-time cohort chat channels with speech bubbles, color-hashed avatars, and keyboard Enter triggers for seamless student-mentor discussions.

🎥

Course-Targeted Live Meets

Faculty schedule live Google Meet mentoring sessions targeted exclusively to enrolled cohorts, with automatic history archiving.

🏆

Gamified Academic Leaderboard

Earn Experience Points (XP) for watching video units, solving exercises, and achieving high scores in unit quizzes to climb the college rank table.

🤖

AI Debugger & Concept Coach

Provides automated code analysis, syntax error explanations, and logical hints when students face compiler obstacles in coding challenges.
